Services

What One Way cleans

Window cleaning covers the glass inside and out, or exterior only, with frames, sills and screens included in the cleaning.

Hard water removal lifts the white mineral spots that sprinklers and rain leave on glass. The job starts with a test spot, so you see what result to expect before the rest of the windows are done.

Pressure washing covers the hard surfaces around a home: driveways, walkways, patios, steps and entryways.

Gutter cleaning clears leaves and debris out by hand, checks that the downspouts flow, and takes care of the debris, so rain drains away from the house instead of over the edge.

One visit can cover several of these at once. The pricing and cost guide explains what each service typically costs in this area and how the free estimate turns that into an exact price for your home.
