Around here

Big trees, big glass, long wet seasons

Bellevue is an Eastside city of wooded neighborhoods. Bridle Trails keeps tall evergreens over most of its lots, Somerset climbs a hill of larger homes above the lake, and even the flatter neighborhoods around Crossroads carry mature street trees. Homes here tend to have more windows, taller windows, and longer driveways than the older housing stock across the lake.

That combination sets the maintenance rhythm. Tree shade keeps concrete damp through the long wet season, and damp concrete grows moss and algae. Tall glass catches pollen and weather film at heights a homeowner cannot safely reach. And because a lot of Bellevue streets are kept to a high standard, sometimes with HOA expectations in the picture, the state of the driveway and the front windows is something homeowners hear about, not just notice themselves.

Service

Pressure washing in Bellevue

This is the lead service in Bellevue for a simple reason: the wet season is long and the shade is deep. A driveway that sits under firs or on the north side of the house stays damp for months, and moss and algae move in as a green to black film. On flat concrete it is mostly an appearance problem. On sloped driveways and front steps it is a grip problem, and it is the reason most people call.

Our pressure washing covers driveways, walkways, patios, steps and entryways, and takes the growth off along with the slick layer it creates. The short version is that washing resets the surface, and shade plus drainage set the clock: most shaded Bellevue driveways want a wash about once a year, timed in spring after the wettest months.

Pressure is matched to the surface. Concrete and pavers get the pressure they can take without marking, and anything softer is a conversation at estimate time rather than a guess on the day.

Window cleaning in Bellevue

Larger Bellevue homes carry a lot of glass: two story entry windows, great room walls facing the trees or the lake, and rows of tall panes that a step ladder from inside does not reach. Our window cleaning covers inside and out or exterior only, with frames, sills and screens included, and the tall interior glass is planned at the estimate rather than improvised on the day.

You can book cleaning inside and out. Big west and south facing windows are the ones the light comes through, and inside film from cooking and fireplaces shows on them first. Exterior only still makes sense for the highest panes and for a mid year refresh between full cleans. Photos of the tall windows help when you ask for the estimate, because pane count and access are what the price is built on.

Under the Eastside tree canopy, pollen season lays a noticeable film in spring. A clean after it settles, plus one more before the dark months, is the rhythm most Bellevue homes settle into.

Questions

Bellevue questions

How often should a Bellevue driveway be pressure washed to keep moss off?

Shaded driveways under mature trees usually need it about once a year. The growth comes back over the wet season because the shade and damp that caused it are still there. Driveways in open sun go longer, and a spring wash after the wet months is the common timing.

Can you clean tall windows inside and out in a larger Bellevue home?

Inside and out cleaning is available for the full house, and tall interior glass, like entry and great room windows, is part of the conversation at estimate time. Access decides the details, so photos of the tall windows help when you ask for the estimate.

Does pressure washing remove the green film on concrete, or just lighten it?

It removes it. The green film is moss and algae growing on and in the surface of the concrete, and washing takes the growth and the slippery layer off. What washing cannot do is change the shade that grows it, so the film returns over time.

Can one visit cover windows, gutters and the driveway?

Yes. A single visit can cover several services, and each is priced on its own factors in the estimate. You can use one visit to get the whole exterior done ahead of guests, a listing or an HOA deadline.
