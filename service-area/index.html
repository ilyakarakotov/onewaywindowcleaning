The corridor

Everett to Bellevue, and everywhere in between

The four named cities anchor the area, and the places in between them are covered too, even though they do not each get a page of their own. If your home sits between Everett and Bellevue, you are in the area this business covers.

Snohomish County

Everett

Everett brings a broad mix of work: older homes near the waterfront where salt air off Port Gardner Bay films the west facing glass, and newer subdivisions on the hills and around Silver Lake with firs and cedars dropping debris into gutters all year. Read the Everett page for how window cleaning and gutter cleaning work there.

Snohomish County

Snohomish

Snohomish pairs a historic district of older homes and older glass with some of the heaviest conifer cover in the county. Needles and leaves fill gutters faster here than anywhere else in the area, and shaded concrete stays damp long enough for moss to take hold. The Snohomish page goes deeper on gutter cleaning and pressure washing under those trees.

King County

Seattle

Seattle homes sit dense and often tall, from Ballard and Queen Anne to West Seattle and Capitol Hill, so a lot of the glass is hard to reach safely from the ground. Summer watering leaves hard water spots on garden facing windows, and spring pollen lays a film over everything. See the Seattle page for window cleaning and hard water removal in the city.

King County

Bellevue

Bellevue means larger homes with a lot of glass, shaded lots under mature trees in neighborhoods like Bridle Trails, and driveways that grow a green, slippery film through the wet season. The Bellevue page covers pressure washing those driveways and walkways, and inside and out window cleaning for bigger homes.
