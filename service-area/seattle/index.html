Around here

Tall houses, street trees and summer watering

Seattle was built up its hills, and the housing shows it. Craftsman homes in the north end, view homes on Queen Anne and in West Seattle, and newer tall, narrow builds tucked between them all share one trait: a lot of the glass is two or three stories up, over ground that slopes away from the house. That is the point where cleaning your own windows stops being a weekend chore and becomes ladder work over a drop.

One Way Window Cleaning LLC provides window cleaning, hard water removal, pressure washing and gutter cleaning for homes in Seattle, WA.

The buildup has a calendar. Spring brings heavy pollen off the street trees and gardens, and it settles on glass as a yellow green film. Summer brings watering, and sprinklers that reach the house leave mineral spots drying on the same panes week after week. Through the wet months, city grime and traffic dust wash down the glass and dry into the gray film most people notice in February.

Service

Window cleaning in Seattle

Our window cleaning covers the glass inside and out or exterior only, with frames, sills and screens included. In Seattle the scope question is usually about reach rather than dirt. Ground floor and second story glass is straightforward. The panes above a garage, over a steep side yard or at the top of a view wall are the ones to talk through at estimate time, because safe access decides what can be cleaned and how.

Inside glass matters more in the city than people expect. Cooking film, fireplace and candle residue, and plain household dust build on the inside of the pane, and clean exterior glass still looks hazy seen through it. A lot of Seattle homes do inside and out for the living spaces and exterior only for the high glass, and that mix is a normal thing to ask for.

Timing follows the pollen. A spring clean once the worst of it settles, then a second clean in late summer or fall, keeps most Seattle homes clear through the gray months when the light matters most.

Hard water removal in Seattle

Seattle gardens get watered hard through the dry summer, and any sprinkler or drip line that reaches the house is spraying the windows too. The water dries, the minerals stay, and by August the lower panes on the garden side can be spotted white. A standard wash does not move it, which is why people often assume the glass is ruined or the seals have failed. Most of the time it is surface deposits, and it is a separate job from cleaning.

Our hard water removal works on those deposits pane by pane. The guide on hard water stains on windows explains how to tell deposits from etching, what vinegar can and cannot do on fresh spots, and where DIY stops being worth it. Two honest limits apply everywhere: deposits left for years can etch into the glass and may not come fully back, and cloudiness between the panes of a double pane window is a failed seal, which no cleaning fixes.

Prevention is mostly aim. A sprinkler head adjusted a few degrees so it stops hitting the house does more for those windows than any cleaning schedule.

Pressure washing in Seattle

Our pressure washing covers driveways, walkways, patios, steps and entryways. In Seattle the worst surfaces are the shaded ones: north side walkways, steps under street trees, and the concrete at the bottom of a sloped lot where runoff keeps it damp. Gutter cleaning in Seattle

Street trees and the maples in Seattle yards fill gutters with leaves in fall, and the big leaf drop is the main event here rather than conifer needles. Our gutter cleaning clears the channels by hand and checks downspout flow. A late fall cleaning after the drop, with a spring check, suits most Seattle homes.

Questions

Seattle questions

Can you reach the windows on a three story Seattle home?

Height and access are the first questions on the estimate for tall homes. A lot of Seattle glass sits on hillside lots or above steep side yards, and what is safely reachable depends on the ground under the window. Tell us the layout when you ask for an estimate and we will confirm what can be done before any work is booked.

What are the white spots on my Seattle windows that washing does not remove?

Those are almost always mineral deposits from irrigation water. Sprinklers and drip lines hitting the same panes all summer dry into white spots that a standard wash cannot lift. That is a hard water job, done separately, and spots that have etched into the glass over years may not come fully back.

How often do Seattle windows need cleaning?

Most Seattle homes land on twice a year: a spring clean after the pollen settles, and a second clean in late summer or fall. Homes near the water or under big street trees film up faster and sometimes add a third.
