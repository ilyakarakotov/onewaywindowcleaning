What a window cleaning includes

A proper window cleaning is more than a spray bottle and a rag. Each pane gets scrubbed to loosen the film, then squeegeed dry and detailed along the edges so you don’t find drips or streaks the next morning when the sun hits the glass.

  • Glass scrubbed and squeegeed, inside, outside or both
  • Edges and corners detailed so nothing dries into streaks
  • Frames and sills wiped down
  • Screens brushed off and put back where they came from
  • A walk-around at the end so you can point out anything we missed

Around here

Why windows get dirty so fast around here

It feels like all the rain in Snohomish County should keep windows clean. It does the opposite. Rain picks up dust, pollen and road grime on the way down, then dries on the glass and leaves the spots behind. Add spring pollen, fall leaves and the green film that grows on anything that stays damp, and windows get dull fast.

If your windows have white spots that a normal wash won’t touch, that’s usually mineral buildup from sprinklers. That’s a different job: see hard water removal.

How often

How often should you get your windows cleaned?

Most homes in the Puget Sound area look their best with a cleaning in spring, once the pollen settles, and again in late summer or fall before the gray months. Homes near the water, a busy road or a lot of trees usually need it more often. Getting your house ready to sell or hosting family for the holidays? That’s a good time too.

Questions

Common questions

Do you clean the inside of the windows too?

Yes. You can book exterior only, or inside and out. Just say which when you ask for an estimate.

Do I need to be home?

For exterior-only jobs, usually not, as long as we can get to the windows. For inside work, someone needs to let us in.

What if it rains on the day?

Light rain usually isn’t a problem. The spots you see after rain come from dirt sitting on the glass, and clean glass sheds water much better. If the weather is really bad, we’ll pick another day together.

How much does window cleaning cost?

It depends on how many windows you have, how many stories, and how easy they are to reach. The estimate is free, and you’ll know the price before any work starts.
