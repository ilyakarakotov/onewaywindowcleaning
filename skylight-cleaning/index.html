Included

What’s included in our skylight cleaning service

One Way Window Cleaning provides a residential skylight cleaning service for homes in Everett, Snohomish, Seattle and Bellevue, WA, and everywhere in between.

A skylight is a window in the roof, and it gets cleaned the same way as the rest of your glass: scrubbed to loosen the film, then squeegeed dry and detailed along the edges. Rooftop skylight cleaning folds into a normal window cleaning visit, not a separate trade and not a separate trip.

  • Exterior glass scrubbed and squeegeed, edges detailed
  • Frames wiped down where they can be reached
  • Interior side cleaned from inside, where it can be reached safely
  • Done in the same visit as your window cleaning

Most homes have fixed or venting skylights; some are domed acrylic. The cleaning is the same for every kind: glass scrubbed and squeegeed, the frame wiped down, and nothing forced open or shut.

Tell us about your skylights when you ask for an estimate: how many there are and roughly where they sit on the roof. We will confirm what is covered for your home before the work starts, in plain terms.

Inside & out

Interior & exterior skylight cleaning

From outside, the glass is scrubbed and squeegeed from a ladder or the roof, the same as any high window on your home. From inside, the glass is cleaned from the room below, which is where the dust and cooking film of the house settle on it.

Most skylights can be done both ways. The exception is an inside pane that sits high over a stairwell or a vaulted ceiling, out of reach from the floor. At the estimate we tell you plainly which of yours can be done from inside, so there are no surprises on the day.

Around here

Why skylights get dirty so fast around here

Rain in Snohomish County does not rinse a skylight clean. It lands on sloped glass, picks up dust and pollen on the way down, and dries where it lands, leaving the film behind. Spring pollen coats it yellow, and through the fall, fir needles and moss collect around the frame and hold damp against the glass.

And because nobody looks at a skylight up close, the buildup goes unnoticed for years. Most people only spot it from inside, on a bright day, when the light coming through looks dull and streaked. A cleaning clears the glass so the room gets its light back.

Access

How we reach them, and when we say no

Outside, skylights get cleaned from a ladder or from the roof itself, the same access used for the rest of the high glass on your home. Inside, the glass is cleaned from the room below, where it can be reached safely.

The pitch of the roof, its condition and where the skylight sits all decide whether it can be reached safely. If one of yours can’t, we say so at the estimate, before any work starts. It is not part of the quote, and nobody finds out halfway through the job.

Limits

What cleaning cannot fix

This service is cleaning only. We do not repair skylights, reseal them or fix their flashing, and cleaning will not stop a leak. A leaking skylight has a failed seal or flashing, and that is a roofer’s job, not ours.

The other limit is fog between the panes. If a skylight looks cloudy but the surface feels smooth, the seal between its two panes has failed and moisture is inside the unit. The surface can be spotless and it will still look fogged. We would rather tell you that before the job than have you find it after.

Cost

What affects the price

The price turns on how many skylights your home has, the pitch of the roof and how safely each one can be reached. Inside and out costs more than exterior only, because it is more glass and more time in the house. Heavy buildup takes longer than a maintenance clean.

Your price comes from the free estimate, after a look at the roof and the glass, and you will know it before any work starts.

Questions

Common questions

How much does skylight cleaning cost?

It depends on how many skylights you have, the pitch of the roof and how safely each one can be reached, whether you want inside and out or exterior only, and how much buildup is on the glass. The estimate is free, and you will know the price before any work starts.

How often should skylights be cleaned?

Once or twice a year suits most homes: once in spring after the pollen settles, and again in late summer or fall before the gray months. Homes under heavy tree cover grow moss and collect needles around the frame faster, and usually need it more often. Most people put skylights on the same schedule as the rest of their window cleaning.

Do you clean skylights from the inside as well?

Yes, where the inside glass can be reached safely from indoors. Some skylights sit high over a stairwell or a vaulted ceiling, out of reach from the floor. At the estimate we tell you plainly which ones we can do from inside, so there are no surprises on the day.

Can you clean skylights on a steep roof?

Some, not all. The pitch of the roof, its condition and where the skylight sits decide whether it can be reached safely from a ladder or the roof itself. If yours can’t, we say so at the estimate, before any work starts, and it isn’t part of the quote.

Will cleaning damage my skylight or roof?

No. Skylights are scrubbed and squeegeed by hand, the same method as the rest of your glass. We never pressure wash a skylight: high pressure can damage the seals and force water past them. Around the skylight, the work stays on the glass and the frame.

Can cleaning fix a leaking skylight?

No. A leak means the seal or the flashing around the skylight has failed, and that is roofing work, not cleaning. If we can see where water is getting in, we will point it out, but the repair itself is a roofer’s job. A pane that stays foggy after the surface is clean usually has a failed seal between the panes, which cleaning can’t change either.
